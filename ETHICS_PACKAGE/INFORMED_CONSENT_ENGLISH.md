# KELASAGARA BIOMATCH · INFORMED CONSENT FORM (ENGLISH)
**Protocol Reference:** IEC-KELASAGARA-2026-001  
**Study Title:** In-Domain Facial Verification Evaluation for Gig and Construction Workers in Karnataka  
**Principal Investigator:** BioMatch Ethics & Compliance Board  

---

## 1. Voluntary Participation
- Your participation in this evaluation is **entirely voluntary**.
- Refusing to participate will **NOT** affect your ability to get jobs, receive payments, or access the Kelasagara platform in any way.
- You may use standard **Document KYC (Voter ID or Aadhaar card manual verification)** at any time.

## 2. What Data is Collected
- Digital facial photographs taken in front of the camera during enrollment.
- Numerical measurements (embeddings) calculated by the model.
- **NO raw facial photographs are permanently stored.** Only mathematical numbers are encrypted and saved.
- **NO Aadhaar biometric data or fingerprint data is accessed or copied.**

## 3. Right to Withdraw and Erasure (DPDP Act 2023)
- You have the absolute right to delete your biometric data at any time.
- Tapping "Delete Biometric Profile" in the app immediately destroys the encryption key (crypto-shredding) and renders your profile permanently erased.
