# PHASE 20.7 PARTICIPANT INFORMATION SHEET
## Information Document for Research Volunteers Participating in Biometric Testing

**Study Title:** Controlled Empirical Validation and Presentation Attack Detection Evaluation of the Kelasagara Biometric Platform  
**Principal Investigator:** Biometric Research Team, University Computer Science Department  
**Project:** Kelasagara Major Project  
**Date:** 2026-11-01  

---

## 1. INVITATION & PURPOSE

You are invited to take part in an academic research study evaluating mobile face recognition and presentation attack detection (spoof prevention) technologies. 

Before you decide whether to participate, it is essential that you understand why the research is being conducted and what it will involve. Please read the following information carefully.

### What is the purpose of the study?
Mobile face recognition allows users to authenticate securely using standard smartphone cameras. However, cameras experience varied lighting, lens blur, and potential presentation attacks (such as photographs or tablet video replays). This study evaluates whether an open-source deep neural model (SFace) accurately and fairly recognizes consenting individuals across separate sessions without confusing them with other people or being tricked by video replays.

---

## 2. VOLUNTARY PARTICIPATION & NO MARKETPLACE IMPACT

- **Participation is Completely Voluntary:** You may choose whether or not to participate. If you decide to participate, you will be given this Information Sheet and asked to sign an Informed Consent Form.
- **Zero Impact on Platform Access:** If you are a worker or hirer on the Kelasagara marketplace, **your participation—or decision not to participate—will have ZERO effect** on your account status, job bookings, earnings, ratings, or customer standing. Research data is completely isolated from production platform accounts.
- **Right to Withdraw:** You may withdraw your consent and exit the study at any time, for any reason, without explanation or penalty. Upon withdrawal, all your recorded research data will be permanently destroyed.

---

## 3. WHAT WILL HAPPEN DURING THE STUDY?

If you agree to participate:
1. **Opaque Identification:** You will be assigned an anonymous, opaque research code (e.g., `SUBJ-C-014`). Your legal name and contact details will never be attached to your facial images or test results.
2. **Short Capture Sessions:** You will be asked to sit in front of an Android smartphone camera for three brief sessions (approximately 2–3 minutes each).
3. **Natural Variations:** You will be photographed under typical lighting conditions (such as indoor room lighting, daylight near a window, or dim ambient lighting). You may be asked to open your eyes, blink naturally, or wear/remove eyeglasses if you wear them.
4. **No Invasive Procedures:** The study uses ordinary consumer smartphone front cameras. There are no flashing strobes, lasers, or invasive physical contacts.

---

## 4. HOW WILL YOUR BIOMETRIC DATA BE PROTECTED?

- **Strict Encryption:** All recorded facial images and derived numerical embeddings are encrypted using AES-256-GCM encryption with hardware-backed keys.
- **Local Storage Only:** Data is stored exclusively on an encrypted, air-gapped local workstation. **Your biometric data will NEVER be uploaded to public cloud databases, GitHub, or commercial AI services (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, or external cloud vision APIs).**
- **No Aadhaar or Government ID Data:** We do not collect or match your face with any government identity database.
- **Automatic Deletion:** In accordance with university research guidelines, all research biometric samples will be permanently purged after 90 days.

---

## 5. RISKS AND BENEFITS

- **Risks:** The physical risk is negligible, equivalent to taking selfies on a personal mobile phone. Privacy risk is minimized through opaque identifiers, local encryption, and strict air-gapping.
- **Benefits:** There is no direct financial compensation. Your contribution directly advances secure, non-punitive biometric authentication for gig economy workers.

---

## 6. CONTACT FOR QUESTIONS

If you have questions regarding your rights as a research participant or wish to withdraw from the study, contact the Principal Investigator via the university computer science ethics liaison.
