## 1. Vernacular Clarity and Consent Architecture

Biometric authentication in the Indian blue-collar context involves workers with diverse literacy levels and primary linguistic fluency in **Kannada**.

### Core Requirements
1. **Plain Language Notice:** Participant consent forms (`ETHICS_PACKAGE/02_INFORMED_CONSENT_KANNADA.md`) avoid dense legal jargon, explaining clearly in Kannada what is scanned, why, and how long it is stored.
2. **Audio/Voice Guidance:** The mobile capture screen provides Kannada voice prompts ("ದಯವಿಟ್ಟು ನೇರವಾಗಿ ನೋಡಿ", "ಕಣ್ಣು ಮಿಟುಕಿಸಿ") to ensure workers understand capture instructions without requiring high text literacy.
3. **No False Government Association:** Consent materials explicitly state that Kelasagara is a private marketplace application and is **not an official UIDAI/Aadhaar system**.

## 2. Meaningful Non-Biometric Alternatives

Under DPDPA 2023 principles and ethical guidelines, consent cannot be considered voluntary if declining biometric processing deprives a worker of their fundamental livelihood.

### The Guaranteed Alternatives Hierarchy
1. **Document KYC:** Uploading a photograph of an official government ID card (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Driver's License) reviewed by a human verification operator within 24 hours.
2. **Supervisor / Physical Verification:** In-person badge verification at local field partner kiosks in Bangalore, Hubli, and Mysore.
3. **OTP-Only Verification:** Job acceptance and attendance confirmation via standard SMS phone OTP.

Any worker who opts out of biometric capture maintains full access to job listings and marketplace earnings without penalty.
